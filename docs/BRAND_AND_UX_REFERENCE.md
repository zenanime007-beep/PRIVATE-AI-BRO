# PRIVATE AI – BRO Official Visual Identity

## Authoritative reference
The user-provided 1536×1536 design board in the project conversation is the authoritative visual reference. Do not replace it with another mascot, orb, palette, or generic AI icon.

## Orb identity
- Transparent glossy glass sphere.
- Bright white/cyan star-like centre.
- Curved translucent blades crossing the centre.
- Electric blue, cyan, teal, magenta and violet.
- Thin luminous orbital rings around the sphere.
- Deep black/navy environment with restrained bloom and reflections.

## Wordmark and tagline
- PRIVATE AI – BRO
- Your Voice. Your Intelligence. Your Private World.
- Created by Ananth.
- Nexora mark appears only as shown in the supplied reference; do not treat Nexora as the app name.

## UI composition
- Floating top assistant pill with the orb and “Tap to open”.
- Six visual states: Idle, Wake Up, Listening, Thinking, Speaking, Sleep.
- Lock-screen concept with “Hey BRO…” activation.
- Expanded translucent assistant panel with orb, waveform, greeting and quick actions.
- Dark navy/black glass surfaces; blue, cyan, violet and magenta highlights.
- Premium, restrained, high-contrast typography and clean spacing.

## Asset usage
- 2D mark: launcher/adaptive icon, notification, splash and small UI.
- Animated orb: main app interface and floating assistant.
- Keep the original orb renderer and map its states to the six visual states.
- Any 3D-looking rendering must be genuinely animated in-app, not a static mock image.
- Provide a lightweight fallback for slower devices.

## Voice activation
- The selected wake phrase is exactly “Hey BRO”.
- Do not enable “Hello BRO” or double-clap as alternate wake triggers.
- Initial target: screen ON. Screen-OFF activation is not part of the confirmed scope.
- Wake-word engine and full speech recognition are separate; do not claim full offline conversation unless independently implemented and tested.

## Quality rule
Do not mark features TESTED until verified on the Vivo Y20G running Android 12.
